13 / HELP

Trade X Help

Phase 1 is designed to grow into the full Trade X intelligence platform.

How do I add a trade?

Open Journal → Add a Trade → record the setup, psychology, result and notes.

Where will Sentrix fit?

Later, Sentrix will use authorized Trade X data to perform commands and automate journal workflows.

Where will Senvora fit?

Later, Senvora will study your authorized history and screenshots to identify patterns and educational insights — not give specific trades.

Is my data connected to a broker?

No. Phase 1 stores demo journal data locally in your browser.
